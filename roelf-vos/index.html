Roelf Vos was born in the Netherlands, in 1921.

He lived through the Second World War…

…and joined the Dutch resistance.

In 1951, he set sail for Tasmania…

…with his wife and their three children.

He arrived with very little.

He worked all sorts of jobs…

…then opened a little grocery store.

And that little store grew…

…into a chain of thirteen supermarkets.

Roelf and his wife adored Switzerland.

Then he had an idea: why not build one back home?

In 1980, he began building his very own Switzerland.

Swiss houses, shops, restaurants, a chapel by the lake, a resort…

And the money?

In 1982, Roelf sold all 13 supermarkets to Woolworths.

Even ordinary homes had to follow strict rules…

…so every house looked like it belonged in the Alps.

Despite the critics, it was a success.

By 1989, the village was largely complete.

In Switzerland, snow is part of every winter.

But here, near Launceston, it almost never snows.

Then, shortly before Roelf passed away in 1992…

…it snowed.

For one brief moment, his little Swiss village lay under snow.

And Roelf got to see it.
