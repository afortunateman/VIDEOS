THE CATERPILLAR AND THE INFINITY STAIRCASE

Voice-over script (narrator)

There was once a caterpillar who was climbing a staircase.
Nobody knew who had built it.
Nobody knew what was at the top.
But the older caterpillars told stories.
"Climb high enough," they said,
"and you'll become a butterfly."
So he climbed.
He climbed past thousands of steps.
Past caterpillars who had given up.
Past the skeletons of those who had died trying.
Some had left messages carved into the walls.
(whispering) Keep going.
The top is worth it.
You're almost there.
One day, he came across a caterpillar descending the staircase.
"What's at the top?" he asked.
"Glory," the caterpillar replied.
"Have you seen it?"
"No."
"But I've heard."
Years passed.
His body grew tired.
His legs weakened.
(a sigh) But he kept climbing
because the thought of remaining a caterpillar terrified him more than the staircase.
One day, he couldn't take it anymore.
He stopped climbing and screamed at the top of his lungs.
Then, from somewhere above, a voice called out.
"You're almost there. Don't give up now. You've come so far!"
But something was different this time.
For the first time, he noticed that every caterpillar was hearing the same voice.
And every single one of them was almost there.
He looked up at the endless staircase.
Then he looked at his little suitcase.
And finally, he sat down.
For a while, he waited.
He wasn't sure what he expected to happen.
But nothing did.
The staircase carried on as it always had.
The other caterpillars did too.
And he was still just as he was before.
There was no revelation or enlightenment.
There were no wings.
And certainly, there was no glory.
But for the first time in his life, he wasn't miserable.
For the first time, he was just there.
And that was enough.
He looked up at the staircase once more.
And for the first time, he wondered...
What if the top was invented by caterpillars who couldn't bear the thought that climbing was all there was?
