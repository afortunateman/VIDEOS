## Visual grammar

- **Camera = mindset.** While he strives, the camera always moves (tracking, cranes, push-ins).
  From the moment he sits down, it locks off. The first static shot in the film is "he sat down".
- **Up vs. out.** For the whole climb, he and the camera face *up* the stairs. At "he was just there"
  he turns and looks *out* at the view for the first time.
- **The suitcase.** Every climber carries one. It's his ambition and his baggage. Always in his right
  hand, on the open (cloud) side of the stairs. Quitters' suitcases lie open and empty; skeletons'
  are dusty. When he sits, he puts it down.
- **Screen direction.** Up the stairs is always screen right. The camera stays on the open (+x) side of
  the staircase for the whole film, so geography never flips (180° rule). In the encounter, the descender
  is always screen right, the hero screen left.
- **Colour script.**
  dawn gold (myth) → firelit night (legend) → clean bright day (hope) → hard afternoon (cost) →
  time-lapse → grey overcast (tired) → red dusk (breaking) → blue night with a warm light from above
  (the voice) → pre-dawn blue (stillness) → morning → golden hour → sunset (enough) → dusk → stars (the question).
- **Physics.** Bodies rest on the steps like rolling balls (each segment touches the tread or the step
  edge, never floats or sinks). Segments keep fixed spacing, so the body stays connected over risers.
  Crawling is a wave from tail to head, and the head only advances as the wave arrives.
  The suitcase hangs straight down from its handle and swings with the stride.
